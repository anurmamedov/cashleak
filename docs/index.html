CashLeak

Most spending apps tell you where your money went.

This one tells you what it cost you.

CashLeak captures what you spend, asks you one question about each purchase — worth it, or leak? — and turns what you'd take back into something concrete. Like the thing you were saving for.

How it works

  • Apple Pay taps arrive automatically through a Shortcuts automation you set up once
  • Everything lands in a Sort queue unconfirmed, and one swipe decides it
  • Your leak total is compared against a goal you name, so the number means something
  • Analysis shows where it goes — by week, by category, by merchant, by day of week

What it doesn't do

  • No bank connection. It never asks for banking credentials and couldn't use them.
  • No subscription. Buy it once.
  • No tracking. No analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no profile of you.
  • No server holding your spending. It stays on your phone and in your own iCloud.

The trade-off is stated honestly in the app: expect roughly 40–60% automatic capture. The rest takes seconds to add.

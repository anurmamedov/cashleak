Why didn't my purchase appear automatically?

Apple gives no app access to Apple Pay transactions. CashLeak works around that with a Shortcuts automation you set up once — one automation covers every card in Wallet — which fires when you tap. It covers roughly half of what most people spend.

It cannot capture physical card taps, in-app and web purchases, e-transfers or cash. Recurring rules handle the predictable remainder — rent, subscriptions — and anything else takes about five seconds to add by hand.

If captures arrive without a shop name, or not at all, open Profile → Apple Pay capture and check step 5: the Amount and Merchant fields in the automation have to be connected, not just filled with placeholder words. Profile → Capture log shows exactly what arrived and when.

What is the difference between spent and leaked?

Spent is every purchase you've confirmed. Leaked is the part of it you marked as one you'd take back.

Nothing is judged automatically. The app never decides from the category or the amount — you swipe each purchase yourself in the Sort queue. That's deliberate: a rule you set once and forget stops telling you anything.

Purchases still waiting in Sort count toward neither figure.

Something is in the wrong place

Everything is editable. Tap any purchase in History or Analysis to change its amount, merchant, date, note, category or verdict, or to delete it. Changing your mind days later is expected, not an error.

My data isn't syncing between devices

Sync uses your own private iCloud database. Check that both devices are signed into the same Apple account and that iCloud Drive is on. Sync needs a network connection on both ends and can take a minute to settle.

How do I get my data out?

Profile → Export CSV gives you every transaction in a standard file you can open in any spreadsheet. No request, no waiting.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Profile → Delete account. Confirm with your password, or with Apple if you signed in with Apple, and the account is deleted. You can also erase all your spending from your iPhone and iCloud at the same time — that option is on by default. Your spending data was never on our side, so there's nothing else for us to remove. You can also email support@karasandlabs.com.

Is my banking information safe?

CashLeak never asks for banking credentials and couldn't use them if you offered. There is no bank connection of any kind. See the privacy policy for what does and doesn't leave your phone.

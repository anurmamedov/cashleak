# CashLeak Privacy Policy

*Last updated 3 October 2026*

CashLeak is designed so that your spending data stays with you. The
transactions, amounts, merchants and judgements you record are stored on your
device and synced only through your own iCloud account. We do not receive
them. The only information we handle is what's needed to run the account you
sign in with.

## What we collect

CashLeak uses Firebase Authentication, a service provided by Google, to operate
sign-in. It holds the information needed to identify your account:

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Email address | Identifies your account and lets you reset your password |
| Password | Stored by the service in hashed form, not as readable text |
| Apple account identifier | Only if you choose Sign in with Apple |
| Account identifier and sign-in times | Created by the service to operate the account |

We do not store your name with your account. Your name is kept in your profile,
on your device and in your iCloud.

As part of operating sign-in and preventing abuse, Firebase also processes
technical information such as IP addresses. Google's handling of this is
described in the [Firebase privacy documentation](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email address, Apple
provides a relay address instead, and we do not receive your real address.

## What we don't collect

We do not receive the following, and the app does not send it to us or to any
other third party:

- Transactions, amounts, merchants, dates and notes
- Your *worth it* and *leak* judgements
- Categories, goals and recurring bills
- Your profile name and profile photo
- The capture log of Apple Pay purchases

This data is stored on your device and synced through **your own private
iCloud database**, which belongs to your Apple account and which we have no
access to. Apple stores iCloud data encrypted. If you turn on Advanced Data
Protection in your iPhone's settings, it is end-to-end encrypted, so only your
trusted devices can read it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## No bank connection

CashLeak does not ask for or use banking credentials, and does not connect to
your bank in any way. Purchases are added through an Apple Shortcuts
automation that you set up yourself, through recurring bills you define, or by
entering them yourself.

## No analytics, advertising or tracking

The app does not include analytics, crash-reporting or advertising tools. We do
not build a profile of you, track you across other apps or websites, or sell
data.

## On your device

Reminders are scheduled on your device by the app; we do not send push
notifications.

If you add a **profile photo**, it is resized on your device and stored with the
rest of your data, on the device and in your iCloud.

If you turn on **Sign in with Face ID**, your password is stored in your
device's Keychain, protected by Face ID, kept on that device only, and not
synced to iCloud or included in backups. It is used only to sign you in.
Turning the setting off, or deleting your account, removes it.

## Your choices

- **Delete your account.** In the app, go to Profile › Delete account. This
  deletes your account record with the sign-in service and, if you use Sign in
  with Apple, revokes CashLeak's access to your Apple ID. You can also email us
  and we will help.
- **Delete your data.** When deleting your account you can also erase your
  spending data from your device and iCloud; this option is on by default. You
  can also delete individual items, delete older purchases, or delete the app.
  Because we don't hold a copy of your spending data, there is nothing on our
  side to erase.
- **Export your data.** Profile › Export CSV exports your purchases at any time.
- **Turn off sync.** If you turn off iCloud for CashLeak in your device's
  settings, the app continues to work on that device only.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over the account
information described above, including access and correction. Contact us and
we will respond.

## Children

CashLeak is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly create
accounts for them.

## Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date above
and let you know in the app before the change takes effect.
